Emelt English Writing: Past Essay Topics

Advanced-level (emelt szint) English written exam, 2017–2026. Every task below is the exact wording from the official papers (source: the PDF supplied by the site owner). Only 10 of the 20 exam periods are in that PDF.

What is original and what is not: the text inside each opened task (the situation, the advert or forum post, the instructions, the word limits and the opening line) is copied word for word from the official papers via the supplied PDF. The task type, style (formal / semi-formal / informal), topic tags, short titles and one-line subjects are this site's own labels to make browsing easier; they are not part of the exam papers. Style is judged from the opening line the paper prescribes: "Dear …" is formal; "Hi …" or "Hello …" to a company or organisation is semi-formal; "Hi …" to a friend and all forum replies are informal. Pictures on the original adverts are not shown.

No "letter to the editor" task appears in the papers included here, so there is no such category.
